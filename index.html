02 / RESEARCH 2023 — 2025

From Classical Verse to Modern Song: Large Language Model–Driven Music Generation of Classical Chinese Poetry

This study applies large language models to generate songs that reimagine ancient Chinese poems in modern musical forms. By integrating symbolic music generation with classical literature education, the system enhances learning interest and reduces memorization burden. A fine-tuned SongComposer model and multidimensional evaluation grounded in music theory confirm its effectiveness, offering innovative tools for cultural preservation and educational practice.
